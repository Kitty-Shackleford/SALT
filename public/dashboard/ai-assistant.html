⚙️ Config Intelligence

Multi-file AI editing via small validated patches, balance passes, config validation and plain-language diff explanations. Patches are checked against the real working copy before anything is stored — apply them from the Suggestions tab.

✏️ Multi-file edit

Pick up to 5 working-copy files and give one instruction that may span several of them (e.g. "lower the spawn rate of all military weapons in both the Chernarus and Enoch types files").

⚖️ Balance pass

Give a goal (e.g. "make early game harder") and pick 2-4 files to coordinate — typically types.xml, events.xml and randompresets.xml. Each suggested patch carries its own rationale.

✅ Config validation

Validate a working copy against the known-good schema + DayZ invariants (unique type/event names, numeric children, sane ranges). The AI explanation is optional — the deterministic report works without any provider.
